Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: Money Simple collects no data. Your accounts stay on your Mac, and nothing is sent over the internet.

The data you enter

The accounts, transactions, categories and notes you enter in Money Simple are stored only on your Mac, in the app's folder. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

We have no access to your data: we cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

No collection, no tracking

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No internet connection: the app works entirely offline.
  • No advertising.
  • No usage statistics, no trackers, no cookies.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Backups

Every day, Money Simple makes a safety copy of your accounts on your Mac (the last 30 days are kept). When you use “Back up my accounts…”, the file is saved wherever you choose. You alone decide where these files go.

Printing

When you print your transactions, macOS sends the document to your printer, as with any other app.

Apple

The app is downloaded and purchased through Apple's Mac App Store. Information related to your purchase is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your personal information.

Children

Money Simple collects no data, including from children.

Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To erase your accounts, simply delete the app and its data folder on your Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
