Frequently asked questions

Where are my accounts stored?

Only on your Mac. Nothing is sent over the internet. To see the folder, open the File → Show the data folder menu.

How do I back up?

Click Options and backup at the top right, then Back up my accounts… (or ⌘S). Choose where to save the file, for example on a USB stick.

Money Simple also makes an automatic safety copy every day on your Mac and keeps the last 30 days.

How do I restore a backup?

Click Options and backup, then Restore a backup… (or ⌘O), and choose your backup file. Note: your current accounts are replaced by the ones in the backup.

How do I move to a new Mac?

On the old Mac, use Back up my accounts… to save to a USB stick. On the new Mac, install Money Simple from the Mac App Store, then use Restore a backup….

What is reconciling with the bank?

It means checking that your accounts match your bank statement. In the Reconciled column, tick each transaction you find on the statement, then click the reconciliation button and type the statement balance. Money Simple tells you whether everything matches, or what the difference is.

The calculation is shown in detail: statement balance, transactions not yet ticked, final balance and account balance. If there is a difference, Money Simple shows you the transactions that may explain it.

How do I make a transfer between my accounts?

In New transaction, choose the Transfer payment method, then choose one of your accounts as the payee. The transfer is then recorded on both accounts.

I deleted a transaction by mistake

Right after deleting, click Cancel in the message that appears at the bottom of the screen. Otherwise, you can restore an automatic backup from the data folder (“sauvegardes” subfolder).

How do I switch the app to French?

Click Options and backup, then choose the Language: English or French. The Mac menus change too.

How do I replay the guided tour?

Click Options and backup then Replay the tutorial, or use the Help → Replay the tutorial menu.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Money Simple works without internet, without signing up and without a subscription.
